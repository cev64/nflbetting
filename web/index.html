How the spread signals have done

Flagged games this season

Power edge: each team's point differential, minus 4 points per turnover of margin (turnovers are mostly luck), blended with last season and pulled toward average. It adds 1.5 points for home field and compares the result to the spread. Turnover fade: backs the team whose season turnover margin is much worse than its opponent's, betting on regression. Signals use only games played before kickoff. The market line is nflverse's number as of the last data refresh (Tuesday) and moves during the week, so compare the model's fair line with your sportsbook's current line.

Red-zone stall offenses

Bend-don't-break defenses

Flagged kickers this season

Trip inside the 40: a drive that ran at least one snap from the opponent's 40 or closer. Red-zone TD %: drives with a snap inside the 20 that ended in a touchdown. Stall offenses reach the 40 at least as often as the league average but score touchdowns on fewer red-zone trips than average; Bend defenses allow at least an average number of trips but fewer red-zone touchdowns than average. Stall / Bend score = how far above average the trips are minus how far above average the red-zone TD rate is (in league standard deviations). The projection multiplies expected trips by expected field-goal tries per trip and the team's make rate, using only games before kickoff. Field goals are noisy, so the probabilities pull the projection most of the way back to league average (see the calibration table). Implied is the team's share of the game total from the spread. The charts and team tables follow the filters; the board and backtest use regular-season games only.
