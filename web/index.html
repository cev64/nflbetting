Trip inside the 40: a drive with a snap from the opponent's 40 or closer. Red-zone TD %: drives with a snap inside the 20 that ended in a touchdown. Stall offenses reach the 40 at least as often as average but score TDs on fewer red-zone trips than average; Bend defenses allow at least an average number of trips but fewer red-zone TDs. The projection multiplies expected trips by expected field-goal tries per trip and the team's make rate, using only games before kickoff; the probabilities pull it most of the way back to league average because field goals are noisy. Implied is the team's share of the game total.
