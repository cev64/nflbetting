Picks

Winner is the ensemble's straight-up pick and its win probability; the tick on the bar is the betting market's. Spread is the side the ensemble expects to cover: edge is how many points its projected margin differs from the line. Models = how many of the individual models lean the same way as the pick. How the picks are made

Many models, one pick

Each model above estimates the chance the home team wins and the expected margin. The stack doesn't simply average them: it learns, from seasons it never trained on, how much to trust each model and in which spots they disagree in useful ways — the models "talking to each other". The winner pick is the side above 50%; the spread pick compares the ensemble's projected margin with the betting line.
