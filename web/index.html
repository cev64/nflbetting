Takeaways = interceptions made + opponent fumbles recovered. Giveaways = interceptions thrown + fumbles lost (including special teams). Fum kept % is the share of a team's own fumbles it recovered; Opp fum rec % is the share of opponent fumbles the team recovered. Fumble recoveries are close to a coin flip league-wide (~50%), so teams far from 50% tend to regress. Click a column to sort, a team to open its matchup view.

How the spread signals have done

Flagged games this season

Power edge: each team's point differential, minus 4 points per turnover of margin (turnovers are mostly luck), blended with last season and pulled toward average. It adds 1.5 points for home field and compares the result to the spread. Turnover fade: backs the team whose season turnover margin is much worse than its opponent's, betting on regression. Signals use only games played before kickoff. The market line is nflverse's number as of the last data refresh (Tuesday) and moves during the week, so compare the model's fair line with your sportsbook's current line.
